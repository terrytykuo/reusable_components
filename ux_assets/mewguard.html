MewGuard — a sentimental-design asset kit

A series of non-functional illustrations and animated assets for MewGuard, the app that helps cat owners check whether a substance is toxic to their cat. They render no data and gate no flow — their only job is to carry the feeling of each moment: an anxious owner deserves calm, a clear verdict deserves warmth, and an emergency deserves a steady hand. Each asset maps to one moment in the MewGuard journey, with the UX payoff it earns.

Persistent avatar · My Cats hero & add-cat flow

Cat avatar — six hand-drawn cats, three poses

The cat an owner keeps is one of six hand-drawn cats (Terry's drawings, shipping since 2026-08-23): 虎斑 tabby · 黑貓 black · 灰貓 grey · 三花 calico · 賓士 tuxedo · 暹羅 siamese. They replaced the parametric line-art cat and its two-axis coat customizer, and flipped the identity model with them — instead of one drawing recoloured by a recipe, the owner picks a finished drawing, and that avatarId is what the users/{uid}/cats doc stores (a legacy coatPattern/coatColor pair, a breed keyword or, last, a name hash still resolve to the nearest of the six, so no cat needed a migration). The six sit drawings share one silhouette; the tail is split out at its junction and buried inside the body at the root, so it can sway without opening a seam, the eyes are split out so they can blink, and everything else is a flat filled overlay painted on top in source order — no outline stroke, which keeps the motion stack to tail sway + breath + blink (plus a hop / joy pop the add-cat flow triggers). Since 2026-10-18 every cat also has a lying and a stretch drawing: the My Cats hero panel seats every cat sitting and, once per visit, lets at most one of them lie down or stretch — same coat, new drawing, never persisted. Small avatars (list rows, chips) are a head crop 0 -8 96 96 of the same sit drawing. Structure maps 1:1 to react-native-svg; every animation is a matrix on a group, mirrored here as a CSS transform.

Cat avatar · Poses
Sit, lying & stretch

One cat, three drawings — each pose has its own silhouette and art box, laid out here exactly as the My Cats hero seats them: a lying or stretching cat is ×1.5 the sit cat's width and shares its ground line. Lying breathes slowly and peeks every ~9 s; stretch runs a 7 s loop — tail lifts 7°, then the body elongates 3.5% from the planted hind paw.

Cat avatar · Motion spec
Three poses, one rhythm family

Pivots are in each pose's own user units (the render box pads the art so a sway or lift never clips). Every move is a rotate or scale on a group — never animated opacity, because react-native-svg nested opacity replaces instead of multiplying. Under reduce-motion the pose stays and the motion stops.

Baked vs. app: the app schedules blinks and peeks at random; a self-contained SVG cannot, so the cards run a fixed 9.6 s blink loop (one single, one double) and a 9 s peek loop. Everything else — angles, scales, durations, easings — is the app's constant verbatim (presets.ts ANIM, DrawnCatPose.tsx LYING / STRETCH). Pose sizing in the hero panel (heroPoses.ts): a lying or stretching cat draws ×1.5 the sit slot's width and borrows its ground line; it is dropped back to sit when it would clip the panel or cover more than 35 % of a neighbour.

Source files (the drawings are the truth, the data is generated): cat_toxin_app/assets/cat-avatar/hand-drawn/{sit · lying/ · stretch/}*.svg → scripts/generate-drawn-cat-{data,poses}.mjs → components/cat-avatar/drawnCatData.ts / drawnCatPoses.ts → DrawnCat.tsx / DrawnCatPose.tsx; the six-way resolver is drawnCats.ts. The cards above are baked from those two data files by ux_assets/tools/bake-drawn-cats.mjs.

MewGuard — sentimental-design asset kit · 32 journey assets + 7 cat-avatar cards + 14 raster-archive cards · all designed assets are pure inline SVG + CSS, dependency-free, and honor prefers-reduced-motion — toggle Reduce motion to preview that still fallback. Copy SVG drops self-animating markup onto your clipboard, or hit the download button to save a standalone .svg file — both embed their own animation <style>, so the asset stays alive the moment you paste or open it. Palette and tone follow the app theme in cat_toxin_app/constants/colors.ts. The Cat avatar section (tag cat-avatar) holds the six hand-drawn cats the app ships (sit) plus one poses card (sit · lying · stretch) — ordinary gallery cards whose SVGs copy and download as standalone self-animating files, baked from the app's path data by ux_assets/tools/bake-drawn-cats.mjs; a motion-spec table and a head-crop legibility mock sit under them. The App raster section (tag app-raster) is the one exception to inline-SVG purity: it archives the real shipped PNGs from cat_toxin_app/assets/, served from the sibling mewguard-raster/ folder.
