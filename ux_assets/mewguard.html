MewGuard — a sentimental-design asset kit

A series of non-functional illustrations and animated assets for MewGuard, the app that helps cat owners check whether a substance is toxic to their cat. They render no data and gate no flow — their only job is to carry the feeling of each moment: an anxious owner deserves calm, a clear verdict deserves warmth, and an emergency deserves a steady hand. Each asset maps to one moment in the MewGuard journey, with the UX payoff it earns.

Interactive prototype · Add-cat flow

Cat avatar — a cat assembled from answers

A parametric SVG cat for the add-cat flow, drawn in line art — style 1a「淨線 Clean」, the direction now shipping in the app. The identity model is the interesting part: every cat is the same drawing — a white body inside a 5 px #1E1B18 outline — and identity comes entirely from the colour laid on top, six pattern switches (stripes / patches / mask / socks / tailTip / muzzle) over three colour slots. So breed drives colour only: there is no breed shape morph, which leaves age and weight as the sole shape inputs and makes the coat reveal a pure fade over an unchanging silhouette. Each answer unlocks one stage — the name wakes the face, age sets head-to-body proportions, weight sets the build, breed paints the coat, and sex deliberately maps to no visual (the cat just reacts). That parameter set is exactly what a users/{uid}/cats doc would store, so the same renderer doubles as the app-wide persistent avatar whenever a cat has no photo. Structure maps ~1:1 to react-native-svg; the transform animations map to Reanimated.

MewGuard — sentimental-design asset kit · 32 journey assets + 8 cat-avatar presets + 14 raster-archive cards · all designed assets are pure inline SVG + CSS, dependency-free, and honor prefers-reduced-motion — toggle Reduce motion to preview that still fallback. Copy SVG drops self-animating markup onto your clipboard, or hit the download button to save a standalone .svg file — both embed their own animation <style>, so the asset stays alive the moment you paste or open it. Palette and tone follow the app theme in cat_toxin_app/constants/colors.ts. The Cat avatar section (tag cat-avatar) is an interactive parametric prototype driven by vanilla JS — its eight breed presets are still ordinary gallery cards whose SVGs copy and download as standalone self-animating files. The App raster section (tag app-raster) is the one exception to inline-SVG purity: it archives the real shipped PNGs from cat_toxin_app/assets/, served from the sibling mewguard-raster/ folder.
