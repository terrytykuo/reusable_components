Mood — an emotional-design asset kit

A series of non-functional illustrations and animated assets. They render no data and gate no flow — their only job is to make the moments around the work feel human: less anxious, more delightful, worth coming back to. Each asset is mapped to one emotional moment in a product journey, with the UX payoff it earns.

Visceral
First-glance warmth — soft shapes, friendly faces, gentle motion.
Behavioral
Motion reassures: progress, success, and patience made visible.
Reflective
Personality the user remembers and re-tells to others.
